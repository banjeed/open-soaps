# Sandalwood Kaleidoscope

A four-color kaleidoscope (column pull-through) bar on a classic olive-coconut-shea base. Scented with P&J Trading Sandalwood fragrance oil. Note: this fragrance accelerates quickly — work fast.

---

## Ingredients

*All weights in grams. Batch size: 700g total oils. Lye amounts verified via SoapCalc at 5% superfat.*

### Oils

| Oil | Weight (g) | % |
|---|---|---|
| Olive oil (pomace) | 350 | 50% |
| Coconut oil | 245 | 35% |
| Shea butter | 70 | 10% |
| Castor oil | 35 | 5% |
| **Total** | **700** | **100%** |

### Lye solution

| Ingredient | Weight (g) |
|---|---|
| Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) | 99.32 |
| Distilled water | 222.09 |

### Fragrance & colorants

*Split batter into 4 equal portions (~175g each) before adding colorants.*

| Portion | Colorant | Amount | Dispersed in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | Blue oxide | 1.5 tsp | Splash of olive oil |
| Green | Blue oxide + yellow oxide | 1 tsp each | Splash of olive oil |
| White | White oxide | 1.5 tsp | Splash of olive oil |
| Black | Carbon black | 2 tsp | Splash of olive oil |

| Fragrance | Amount | When added |
|---|---|---|
| P&J Trading Sandalwood FO | 10g | Light trace, split across all four portions |

---

## Equipment

- Digital scale
- Two stainless steel or HDPE containers
- Stick blender
- Thermometer
- Silicone spatulas (at least 4)
- 4 separate pouring containers
- Tesscco silicone column mold

---

## Process

1. Weigh all oils. Melt shea butter and coconut oil gently; combine with olive and castor oils.
2. In a ventilated area, slowly add lye to distilled water. Stir until dissolved. Set aside to cool.
3. Pre-disperse all colorants in a small splash of olive oil each. Set aside.
4. When oils and lye solution are both 90–110°F and within ~10° of each other, pour lye into oils.
5. Stick blend to a very light trace — thinner is better for this technique, as the batter thickens further during coloring.
6. Add all 10g of sandalwood fragrance oil to the full batch. Stick blend briefly to incorporate.
7. Divide batter into 4 equal portions (~175g each).
8. Stir a pre-dispersed colorant into each portion. Do not stick blend — hand stir only to preserve fluidity.
9. Pour into the column mold in alternating colors to build the kaleidoscope pattern.
10. Perform pull-through with a skewer or chopstick.
11. Cover and insulate for 24–48 hours.
12. Unmold carefully (column molds can be fragile to unmold). Cut.
13. Cure on a rack with airflow for **6 weeks** minimum.

---

## Notes

**Acceleration warning:** Sandalwood FO (P&J Trading) accelerates this batter quickly. Have all four pouring containers ready and colorants pre-dispersed before you add fragrance. Work at the coolest end of the temperature window (90–95°F) to slow trace.

**Technique note:** This is a high-skill pour. If the batter thickens too fast to pour cleanly, a simpler in-the-pot swirl is a good fallback.
