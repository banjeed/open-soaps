# Lemon Poppy Seed

A crisp, clean bar built on a classic olive-coconut base. Poppy seeds provide light visual texture in the finished bar. Scented with lemon essential oil — note that citrus EOs can fade; see process notes below.

---

## Ingredients

*All weights in grams. Batch size: 500g total oils.*

### Oils

| Oil | Weight (g) | % |
|---|---|---|
| Olive oil | 250 | 50% |
| Coconut oil (76°) | 200 | 40% |
| Castor oil | 50 | 10% |

### Lye solution

| Ingredient | Weight (g) |
|---|---|
| Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) | 69.8 |
| Distilled water | 190 |

*Lye calculated at 5% superfat, 38% water ratio using SoapCalc. Always verify with your own calculator before proceeding.*

### Additives

| Ingredient | Amount | When added |
|---|---|---|
| Lemon essential oil (10x or folded) | 17.5g | Light trace |
| Poppy seeds | 2 tbsp | Light trace |

---

## Equipment

- Digital scale (0.1g precision)
- Two stainless steel or HDPE containers
- Stick blender
- Thermometer
- Silicone spatula
- Silicone loaf mold (approx. 10" × 3.5")

---

## Process

1. Weigh all oils. Gently melt coconut oil and combine with olive and castor oils.
2. In a separate heat-safe container outdoors or in a ventilated space, slowly add lye to distilled water. Stir until fully dissolved. The solution will heat rapidly — set aside to cool.
3. Allow both oil mixture and lye solution to cool to 90–110°F, within ~10° of each other.
4. Slowly pour lye solution into oils while stick blending in short bursts.
5. Blend to light trace — batter should leave a faint trail when dripped on the surface.
6. Add lemon essential oil and stir by hand to incorporate. Add poppy seeds and stir to distribute.
7. Pour into mold.
8. Cover with a piece of cardboard and insulate with a towel for 24–48 hours.
9. Unmold after 48–72 hours. Cut into 8 bars.
10. Cure on a rack with airflow for **6 weeks** minimum.

---

## Notes

**On lemon scent longevity:** Standard lemon essential oil fades significantly during cure. Use a 10x or folded lemon EO for better retention, or accept a lightly scented finished bar. A lemon-compatible fragrance oil will hold better if scent strength matters.

**On acceleration:** This recipe behaves well with the oils listed. Adding fragrance below 100°F reduces acceleration risk.

**Poppy seed distribution:** Add seeds last and stir by hand — the stick blender will break them down.
