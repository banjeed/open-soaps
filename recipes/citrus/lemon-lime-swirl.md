# Lemon Lime Swirl

A bright two-scent, two-color bar on an olive-coconut-shea base. Lemon and lime fragrance oils (Salubrito) are each paired with a coordinating oxide colorant and poured into a 42oz loaf mold.

---

## Ingredients

*All weights in grams. Batch size: 700g total oils. Lye amounts verified via SoapCalc at 5% superfat.*

### Oils

| Oil | Weight (g) | % |
|---|---|---|
| Olive oil (pomace) | 350 | 50% |
| Coconut oil | 245 | 35% |
| Shea butter | 70 | 10% |
| Castor oil | 35 | 5% |
| **Total** | **700** | **100%** |

### Lye solution

| Ingredient | Weight (g) |
|---|---|
| Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) | 99.32 |
| Distilled water | 222.09 |

### Fragrance & colorants

*Split batter into 2 equal portions (~350g each) before adding colorants and fragrance.*

| Portion | Fragrance | Amount | Colorant | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lemon | Salubrito Lemon FO | 10g | Yellow oxide | 1 tsp |
| Lime | Salubrito Lime FO | 10g | Blue oxide + yellow oxide | 0.5 tsp each |

*Colorants pre-dispersed in a small splash of olive oil.*

---

## Equipment

- Digital scale
- Two stainless steel or HDPE containers
- Stick blender
- Thermometer
- Silicone spatulas
- 2 separate pouring containers
- 42oz (1200ml) loaf mold

---

## Process

1. Weigh all oils. Melt shea butter and coconut oil gently; combine with olive and castor oils.
2. In a ventilated area, slowly add lye to distilled water. Stir until dissolved. Set aside to cool.
3. Pre-disperse colorants in a small splash of olive oil each. Set aside.
4. When oils and lye solution are both 90–110°F and within ~10° of each other, pour lye into oils.
5. Stick blend to a light trace.
6. Divide batter into two equal portions (~350g each).
7. To the lemon portion: add 10g Salubrito Lemon FO and stir in yellow oxide. Mix to incorporate.
8. To the lime portion: add 10g Salubrito Lime FO and stir in blue + yellow oxide blend. Mix to incorporate.
9. Pour both portions into the 42oz loaf mold in alternating pours or a side-by-side pour to create swirl.
10. Cover and insulate for 24–48 hours.
11. Unmold after 48–72 hours. Cut into bars.
12. Cure on a rack with airflow for **6 weeks** minimum.

---

## Notes

**Colorant note:** Blue + yellow oxide at 0.5 tsp each produces a muted sage-green. Adjust ratio toward yellow for a brighter lime green.

**Scent note:** Citrus fragrance oils can fade during cure. Salubrito Lime and Lemon FOs were used rather than essential oils for better retention.

**Mold note:** This recipe fills a 42oz / 1200ml loaf mold comfortably.
