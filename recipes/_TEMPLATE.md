## Process

1. Weigh all oils. Melt solid oils gently and combine with liquid oils.
2. In a separate heat-safe container, slowly add lye to distilled water. Stir until dissolved.
3. Allow both to cool to 90–110°F, within ~10° of each other.
4. Slowly pour lye solution into oils.
5. Stick blend to light trace (batter leaves a faint trail when dripped on surface).
6. Add fragrance oil and any colorants. Stick blend briefly to incorporate.
7. Pour into mold.
8. Cover and insulate for 24–48 hours.
9. Unmold after 24–72 hours. Cut into bars.
10. Cure on a rack with airflow for **6 weeks** minimum.
