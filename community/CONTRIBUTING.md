# Contributing a recipe

Thank you for sharing your work. This is a maker-to-maker resource and every contribution helps.

---

## Before you submit

- The recipe must be your own original formulation or clearly derived from a public domain source with attribution.
- Run your lye amounts through a lye calculator and verify them before submitting.
- The recipe must have been made and tested at least once by you.
- Do not include therapeutic, cosmetic, or skin-benefit claims in recipe text. Describe what the recipe *contains* and *how it behaves in process* — not what it does to skin.

---

## File format

Copy the template from `recipes/_TEMPLATE.md` and fill it in. Place your file in the correct scent family subfolder.

Filename format: `primary-scent-descriptor.md` — lowercase, hyphenated, descriptive.

Examples:
- `lavender-oat.md`
- `lemon-clay.md`
- `unscented-shea-basic.md`

---

## Submitting

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your recipe file in the correct folder.
3. Open a pull request with a brief description of the recipe.
4. A maintainer will review for format and safety — not for whether the recipe is "good."

---

## Community norms

- Be generous with credit. If you adapted someone else's recipe, say so.
- Be specific. Vague notes like "add fragrance" are less useful than "add 28g fragrance oil at light trace."
- Be honest about failures. A note saying "this riced when fragrance was added above 80°F" is valuable.
- Do not submit recipes that make health or skin claims of any kind.
